# Avicenna: "Know the best clinical trial for your patient"

## Mission
Build the best system for knowing which clinical trial is best for a given patient. It is a hackathon project (Rakathon 2026, challenge #1 "AI Trial Matching", FN Motol and Homolka, Oncology Clinic), with a submission deadline of Saturday 13:00 Europe/Prague.

## Context from the client brief
- The tool should convert trial inclusion/exclusion criteria into structured form, compare them with anonymised patient data, and give each result a match score with an explanation (criterion met, not met, or data missing).
- What the client really wants: to be alerted that a patient meets trial criteria before anyone thinks of it.
- Data: a shared Motol dataset of anonymised reports, CT text descriptions, lab results, billed procedures and drugs, and other hospital-system data on currently treated oncology patients. No images. Also a list of trials recruiting at the Oncology Clinic, and clinicaltrials.gov.

## Loose focus
- Location: Prague first, then Brno and Ostrava, then the rest of Czech republic, then the world
