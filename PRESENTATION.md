EVERY DOCTOR WANTS THE BEST FOR THEIR PATIENT.

IN THE MOST DIFFICULT SITUATIONS, WHEN EVERYTHING IS ON THE LINE THAT IS USUALLY A CLINICAL TRIAL. WE BELIEVE THE BEST PATH SHOULD BE SIMPLE TO TAKE, EVEN IF IT’S HARD TO GO THROUGH,

THAT IS WHY WE BUILT AVICENNA. IT FINDS CLINICAL TRIALS AND KEEPS A MASSIVE DATABASE OF THEM. AS WE SPEAK IT HOLDS DATA OF CLINICAL TRIALS IN PRAGUE, BRNO, OSTRAVA, CZECH REPUBLIC, GERMANY, ITALY, THE WHOLE EUROPEAN CONTINENT AND EVEN THE REST OF THE WORLD.

BUT ASKING DOCTORS TO GO THROUGH ALL THIS DATA WOULD NOT BE SIMPLE. WE BUILT A MATCHING ENGINE, ON TOP OF INTERSYSTEMS TECHNOLOGY THAT ALLOWS US TO MAKE THIS MATCHING WITHOUT EVER READING CONFIDENTIAL PATIENT DATA OFF OF THE HOSPITAL SERVERS, MEANING WE CREATE LOWER RISK.
