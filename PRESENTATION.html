Challenge #1 · AI Trial Matching

The belief

Every doctor wants the best for their patient.

In the most difficult situations — when everything is on the line — that is usually a clinical trial.

The promise

The best path should be simple to take.

Even when the journey through treatment is hard. Finding the right trial should not be.

That is why we built Avicenna

A living map of clinical trials.

As we speak, it holds trial data across Prague, Brno, Ostrava, the Czech Republic, Germany, Italy, Europe — and the rest of the world.

Prague Brno · Ostrava Czechia Europe World

The problem with the catalog

Doctors should not have to read all of it.

We built a matching engine on InterSystems technology — so comparison happens without confidential patient data ever leaving hospital servers.

Lower cyber risk. Clearer go-to-market. Matching stays where the patient is.
01
Cloud · trial side Structure public criteria (CTIS, ClinicalTrials.gov) into versioned packs.
02
Hospital · InterSystems IRIS Patient facts stay local. Deterministic matching. Explainable results.
03
Clinician Alerted to candidates — before anyone thinks to look.

How matching reads

Criterion by criterion. Honest about gaps.

Not a black-box score. Each inclusion and exclusion resolves to met, not met, or data missing — with the protocol text still visible.

Met Age ≥ 18 · documented from demographics
Not met Prior SERD for advanced disease · exclusion triggered
Data missing ECOG at screening · needs chart review
